This is a personal website to showcase some of my projects I've been involved in during my Bachelor's and Master's degree and, hopefully, get me a job in machine learning.

My primary interest lies in Computer Vision, how this fundamentally is inspired by the human vision apparatus and what can we improve in it. I take a special interest in Explainability and Interpretabily in the context of vision as I find it necessary to escape the Black Box dogma (it works, so I don't care how), especially now when we are bringing ML and AI into social and life sciences.

I'm currently pursuing a highly interdisiciplinary M.Sc. at <a href="https://www.uni-heidelberg.de/en">University of Heidelberg</a>, where I focus on applications of AIML in social and life sciences. At the same time, I am pursuing the <a href="https://www.heiskills.uni-heidelberg.de/en/certificates-0/certificate-of-advanced-studies-cas-fur-masterdoktorandinnen/cas-cognitive-science">Certificate of Advanced Studies in Cognitive Science</a>, a complementary interdisciplinary program to my degree, where I focus on aspects of Cognitive Science such as Computational Cognitive Science, fMRI Analysis, Linguistics, Philosophy and Behavioral Modeling.

For the past year I have been working as an intern in the Machine Learning Human Behavior laboratory where I deal (as the name suggests) with Human Behavioral Modeling and Behavior Forecasting in the context of Repetead Trust Games. To this end I use both traditional statistical methods (such as Bayesian hierarchical modeling, Hidden Markov Chains etc.) and advanced Dynamical System Reconstruction models (such as shPLRNNs or AL-RNNs), with the goal of developing control mechanisms that can drive the agent behavior into desired equilibrium trust states (which I hope can contribute to individualized therapeutical applications or psychopathology). 

In my free time I enjoy philosophy, psychology, literature and art.
